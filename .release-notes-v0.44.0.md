### Added

- **SVG uses the room available in the viewer.** A Mermaid diagram, or an SVG image on a line of its own, is drawn at its natural width, or wider until its small print reaches 12px or its typical text reaches 16px, whichever comes first. It is never wider than the panel less its gutters, and one wider than the text column is centred on the column, as a wide table is. A wide diagram is no longer squeezed into the 760px column. A drawing whose text is already that large, or that has no text, stays at its natural width.
- **Documents open inside T3 Code.** In the plugin build of [T3 Code](https://github.com/marcelocantos/t3code), a Markdown file link clicked in a chat opens in the browser when you are at the machine running the viewer, and as a card inside T3 on any other client, with edit and save. `vellum serve-view` advertises itself to T3 while it runs; enable **Vellum** once under **Settings → Plugins**. The card needs a T3 build with document cards (after 0.7.0).
- **`serve-view --t3-addr host:port|off`** (or `VELLUM_T3_ADDR`) places or disables the listener T3 reaches. It defaults to the view port plus one on macOS and is off elsewhere.

### Changed

- **`serve-view` stops cleanly on SIGINT and SIGTERM.**
- **The viewer's write requests can name their stamp in the query** (`?stamp=`), and a 409 ends with a `stamp <stamp>` line. The `X-Vellum-Stamp` header still works.

### Unchanged

- An image inside a sentence, an image given a `width` or `height`, a diagram with a `vellum:scale` hint, and an SVG with no `viewBox` keep their size. Print and PDF output are the same as before.
