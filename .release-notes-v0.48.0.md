### Fixed

- **An SVG held to its container still grows to reading size.** A drawing or SVG image styled `max-width: 100%; height: auto` (as many exporters write it) was treated as sized by its author and left at its natural width. A style that only keeps a drawing inside its container no longer counts as sizing it: `max-width` of 100% or more (or `none`), and `width` or `height` of `auto`. Such a drawing grows like one with no style, up to the panel's width. Any other size, and anything marked `!important`, is still left as written.
